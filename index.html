01 / About

Curiosity is my method. Change is my ambition.

I am Arathy Aluckal — a social designer and researcher working at the intersection of inclusion, systems thinking, public life and visual communication.

My practice moves between field research and design-making: listening to people, mapping relationships, uncovering structural gaps and translating insights into products, services, campaigns and experiences. I am especially interested in how design can become a tool for social change rather than simply a layer of polish.

03 / How I work
